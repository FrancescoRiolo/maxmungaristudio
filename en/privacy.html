2. Data Collected and Purpose

This website collects only the personal data (first name, last name, phone number, email address) that users voluntarily provide when contacting the data controller through the listed contact details. Such data is processed solely for the purpose of responding to the requests received.

Legal basis for processing: performance of pre-contractual measures at the request of the data subject (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and legitimate interest of the controller in responding to received communications (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Retention period: data is retained for the time strictly necessary to handle the request and, unless required by law, no longer than 24 months from the last contact.

3. Cookies and Browsing Data

This site does not use profiling cookies or traffic analytics systems. The only technical data stored locally is a value in sessionStorage (not a cookie) used to manage the welcome screen: it is automatically deleted when the browser is closed and is exempt from consent requirements under applicable regulations.

Vimeo: the site embeds a video through the Vimeo player with Do Not Track mode enabled (dnt=1). In this mode, Vimeo does not set tracking cookies or collect user data for advertising purposes. For more information: vimeo.com/privacy.

Google Fonts: typefaces are self-hosted directly on the site's servers. No data is transmitted to Google LLC for font loading.

Google Maps: the site includes an interactive map provided by Google LLC (USA). The map is not loaded automatically: a consent panel is displayed and the Google Maps iframe is activated only upon explicit user action ("Accept and load map"). Consent is stored in the browser (localStorage) and can be revoked by clearing browsing data. If consent is not given, the location can be opened directly in Google Maps without the site transmitting any data. For more information: policies.google.com/privacy.

The site contains links to social channels (Instagram, Facebook, YouTube). Once clicked, users access third-party platforms beyond the controller's control, who cannot be held responsible for data processing carried out by such parties. Users are encouraged to consult the respective privacy policies.

4. Data Subject Rights

To exercise your rights, write to: info@maxmungaristudio.com
