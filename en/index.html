Guitarist — Producer — Sound Engineer

MAX MUNGARI

Guitarist, music producer and sound engineer specialising in Mixing & Mastering.

Born in Crotone in 1976, he found in the guitar his instrument of choice from an early age — the one through which he could best express his creative potential and his deep passion for sound and the art of music.

Through years of studio experience, he gradually developed as an arranger and music producer, deepening his study of music production before going on to specialise in audio mixing and mastering.

He founded Orange Records Music Label and established Studio 1976, where he produces music for artists across a wide range of genres — with a particular affinity for rock and pop, always immersed in and inspired by contemporary music.

He also works as a live and session guitarist, contributing to third-party productions.

Mixing & Mastering

MIXING &
MASTERING.

What is Mixing?

The process of balancing, equalising and dynamically treating individual tracks: every element finds its place to create a clear, cohesive sound with immediate emotional impact.

What is Mastering?

The final stage of the audio chain: it optimises the mix for maximum translatability across every format and platform — streaming, radio, vinyl, CD — while preserving the sonic identity of the track.

What does this service include?

Mixing Standard, Stem Mixing, Advanced Stem Mixing, Mastering Standard, Stem Mastering and Advanced Stem Mastering — every level of processing to meet any production need.

Music Production

MUSIC
PRODUCTION.

What is Music Production?

Music production is the creative process that guides every artist from the initial idea to the final master. Starting from a rough demo, a reference or even just a mood: Max builds the arrangement, records the instrumental parts and drives every decision with instinct and experience, giving the project a unique and unmistakable sonic signature.

What does this service include?

Composition, arrangement, professional recording at Studio 1976, editing, sound design, mixing and mastering — from the first note to the finished product ready for distribution. Available in studio in Crotone or entirely online.
